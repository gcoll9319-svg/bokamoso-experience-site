We Don't Just Speak Youth Empowerment — We Live It

Bokamoso Experience NPC is a community-driven sports club based in Kagiso, Krugersdorp. We give children and young people a safe, exciting space to play, build confidence, and connect — through go-karting and community events. As a registered NPC, every booking and event also supports our wider youth empowerment work, including outreach days at local primary schools.

What We Offer

Go-Kart Sessions

Supervised go-kart driving for kids and groups, on-site in Kagiso.

School & Youth Days

Fun-filled outreach days bringing go-karts and activities directly to schools.

Group & Party Bookings

Birthdays, school groups, and corporate youth-day bookings welcome.
